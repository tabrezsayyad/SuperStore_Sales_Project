# SuperStore-Sales-Dashboard

**1. Project Title**
SuperStore Sales Dashboard – Power BI

**2. Short Description**
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze SuperStore sales and profitability. It provides insights into sales trends, customer segments, product categories, shipping modes, and regional performance.

**3. Tech Stack**
Power BI – Main data visualization platform used for dashboard creation
Power Query – Used for data cleaning and transformation
DAX – Used for creating calculated measures and KPIs
Data Visualization – Used to present business insights through interactive charts and visuals
File Format – Excel (.xlsx)

**4. Data Source**
SuperStore Sales Dataset

The dataset contains sales transactions including order details, customers, products, sales, profit, quantity, shipping information, and regional data.


**6. Featured / Highlights**
📊 Total Sales: 1.51M
💰 Total Profit: 170.50K
📦 Total Quantity: 22K
🚚 Average Delivery: 3.92 Days
👥 Top Segment: Consumer
📱 Top Sub-Category: Phones
🚛 Preferred Ship Mode: Standard Class
📈 Monthly Sales & Profit: YoY Analysis
🌎 Regional & State-Level Sales & Profit Analysis
🛍️ Category & Sub-Category Performance Analysis

Business Problem:
The business needs to understand its sales and profit performance across different regions, product categories, customer segments, and shipping modes.

The main objective is to identify sales trends, profitable and loss-making areas, top-performing products, and opportunities for improving overall business performance.
